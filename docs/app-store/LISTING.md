## Notes for App Review

> Covera stores the user's own health insurance policy documents and quotes them back with the page and exact sentence for every figure. It does not provide medical, legal or insurance advice, and it never states a figure it cannot trace to the user's document. A mandatory onboarding screen and a disclaimer on every answer say so.
>
> Demo account (policies already uploaded):

>
> The sample policies are fictional documents created for review.

Before submitting: create the review account on the production server, upload
two or three fictional policies to it, and fill in the two blanks above.
