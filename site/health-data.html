Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This policy applies to consumer health data as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act, Nevada Senate Bill 370 and similar US state laws. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

What consumer health data we collect

Health insurance documents describe health. The consumer health data Clausa collects is:

  • The contents of insurance policies, claim documents and receipts you upload, which may reveal health conditions, treatments, procedures, medications, providers and pregnancy-related cover.
  • Information read from those documents: coverage terms, limits, waiting periods, exclusions and deadlines.
  • The questions you ask in the app and the answers you tap when getting a plan — for example, that you or a family member is planning surgery. These are used to produce a reply and are not stored by us afterwards.

Why we collect it

Only to provide the service you ask for: storing your policies, reading them back to you with citations, answering your questions and drafting a plan. We do not use consumer health data for advertising, marketing, profiling, research or any other purpose.

Where it comes from

Only from you — the documents you upload and what you type or tap in the app. We do not obtain health data from insurers, providers, data brokers or any other source.

Consent

We collect consumer health data only after you give consent in the app, before your first upload. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data or account in the app. We do not share consumer health data with third parties, and we would ask for a separate consent before ever doing so. We never sell consumer health data.

Who receives it

Only our processors, which handle it on our instructions under contract and may not use it for their own purposes:

  • Google LLC (Gemini API) — reads documents and drafts answers. Its paid-service terms prohibit using the content to train models.
  • Our hosting provider — hosts our servers and encrypted storage. We will name it here before Clausa is released.

We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data. We do not use geofencing around healthcare facilities.

Your rights

  • Confirm and access — Account → Export everything shows you all consumer health data we hold, and the list above names every entity that receives it.
  • Delete — Account → Delete my account deletes it from our systems immediately, and our processors hold no copy we control beyond short backup and abuse-monitoring windows.
  • Withdraw consent — by deleting your data or account.

You can also write to covera.privacy@gmail.com. We respond within 45 days. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision with the word "Appeal"; we will answer the appeal within 45 days, and if you are unhappy with the result you may contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint or the attorney general of your state.

Clausa is not a doctor, a lawyer or a licensed insurance agent. It quotes your own documents; your insurer decides what is covered.
