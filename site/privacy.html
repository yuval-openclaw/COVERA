Privacy Policy

Covera keeps your health insurance policies and reads them back to you. That means it holds health information, and this page says exactly what happens to it.

Last updated: 22 September 2026 · Effective from the date the app is first made available

The short version. We store the documents you upload so the app can quote them back to you. To read them we send their contents to Google's Gemini API, under terms that forbid Google from using them to train its models. We don't sell your data, share it with insurers or employers, show you ads or track you. You can export everything or delete your account from inside the app at any time, and deletion is immediate.

1. Who is responsible

Covera is operated by Eyal Baruch, a private individual based in Israel ("we", "us"). We are the controller of your personal data — the party that decides why and how it is processed — and the owner of the database for the purposes of Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981.

Contact: covera.privacy@gmail.com

3. What we do not collect

  • No location, contacts, photos library, microphone or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party trackers or ad networks in the app.
  • We do not buy data about you from anyone, and we do not combine your data with other sources.

5. Who processes it for us

We share your data only with service providers that process it on our instructions, under written terms that bind them to confidentiality and security:

  • Google LLC (Gemini API) — reads your documents, transcribes scanned pages, answers questions, builds plans and creates search vectors. We use Google's paid Gemini API service, whose terms prohibit Google from using submitted content to improve its products or train its models. Google may keep request data briefly to detect abuse, as its terms describe.
  • Google (Sign in with Google) — only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Our hosting provider — hosts our servers, database and encrypted file storage. Covera is not yet released; we will name the provider and the region where your data is stored here before it is.
  • Apple — distributes the app. Apple's own privacy policy covers what it collects through the App Store.

We do not share your data with insurers, employers, brokers, advertisers or data brokers. We would disclose it to authorities only where the law requires it, and we would tell you unless the law forbids that.

6. International transfers

We are based in Israel, which the European Commission recognises as providing adequate protection for personal data. Our processors may handle data in the United States and elsewhere. Where data from the EU, the UK or Switzerland goes to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified (Google is), or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the UK Addendum. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, documents and information read from them: until you delete them or your account. Deletion from our live systems is immediate.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in encrypted backups for up to 30 days, after which it is overwritten. It is not used or restored in that time except to recover from a failure.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.
  • Sign-in sessions: until you sign out, or 30 days after you last used the app.

8. How it is protected

  • Uploaded files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are written to storage, and all traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes, and sign-in sessions only as hashes of their tokens, so a copy of our database would reveal neither.
  • Every request for your data is scoped to your account, and an automated test fails our build if any database query for user data leaves that scope out.
  • On your phone, your sign-in token is kept in the iOS Keychain and is not included in iCloud backups.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will notify the authorities and you as required by law, and without undue delay.

9. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can do the following — the first two directly in the app, instantly:

  • Get a copy. Account → Export everything gives you one file with your account, your policies and everything read from them, citations included.
  • Delete. Account → Delete my account removes your account, your files and everything read from them, immediately and permanently. No support ticket, no waiting period.
  • Correct. If something read from a document is wrong, delete the document and upload a clearer copy, or write to us.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, by deleting your data or your account.
  • Object or restrict processing, and take your data elsewhere (portability) — the export file is in a standard machine-readable format.
  • Complain to a data protection authority — see section 14.

For anything the app cannot do itself, write to covera.privacy@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, and we will not treat you differently for exercising any right.

10. Children and family members

Covera is for adults. You must be 18 or older to use it, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Policies often cover a whole family, so documents you upload may include a child's or relative's details. Upload them only if you are their parent, guardian or otherwise entitled to manage their insurance. If you believe a child has used Covera, contact us and we will delete the account.

11. How the AI is used

Covera uses an AI model to read your documents and draft answers. Every figure it shows must be traced to a page and an exact quote in your own document; anything it cannot trace is withheld rather than shown. The AI makes no decisions about you — it does not decide coverage, eligibility or anything with a legal or similarly significant effect. Your insurer makes those decisions. AI can still misread a document, which is why every figure shows its source so you can check it.

12. Region-specific information

Israel

We process your data in accordance with the Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, its regulations (including the Protection of Privacy (Data Security) Regulations, 5777-2017) and Amendment No. 13. You have the right to review the information about you held in our database and to ask for it to be corrected or deleted. The supervising authority is the Privacy Protection Authority.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

Because we are not established in the EU or the UK, the law requires us to appoint representatives there whom you can contact instead of us (GDPR and UK GDPR, Article 27). Covera is not yet available in the EU or the UK. We will appoint both representatives and list their names and addresses here before it is.

You may complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

United States

We do not sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling. Residents of Washington, Nevada and other states with consumer health data laws should also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. Residents of California and other states with comprehensive privacy laws have rights to know, access, correct, delete and port their data and to limit the use of sensitive information; the controls in section 9 exercise them, or write to us. You may use an authorised agent, and we will verify requests by confirming control of the account.

Everywhere else

The rights in section 9 apply to everyone, whatever local law requires.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change affects how your health information is used, we will tell you in the app and ask for your consent again before it applies to you.
