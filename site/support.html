Support

Write to covera.privacy@gmail.com with any question or problem. We reply within 5 business days.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number first. Covera cannot help with medical care, and questions about what your policy covers are decided by your insurer.

Common questions

A figure in my policy is not shown.

Covera only shows a figure it can match, word for word, to a page of your document. If it cannot — a poor scan, an unusual layout — it leaves the figure out and suggests you check the document or ask your insurer. That is deliberate: a missing figure is safer than a wrong one.

My document could not be read.

Upload the original PDF from your insurer rather than a photo or a scan where you can. If a scan is all you have, make sure the page is flat, well lit and in focus.

Does Covera decide what I am covered for?

No. Covera reads your documents back to you. Only your insurer decides what is covered and how much it pays.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what happens to your documents.

Covera is not a doctor, a lawyer or a licensed insurance agent. It quotes your own documents; your insurer decides what is covered.
