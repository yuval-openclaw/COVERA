Accessibility Statement

We want everyone to be able to read their own insurance, including people who use a screen reader, larger text or other assistive technology.

Last reviewed: 21 September 2026

This website

This site is built to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA, and with Israeli Standard SI 5568 as required by the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adaptations) Regulations, 5773-2013. In particular:

  • Pages use semantic headings, landmarks and a "skip to content" link.
  • Everything can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • All text meets AA contrast ratios against the background.
  • Text resizes up to 200% without loss of content, and the layout adapts to narrow screens.
  • There is no autoplaying media, flashing content or time limit.

The Covera app

  • Supports VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and Reduce Motion.
  • Is available in ten languages, with Hebrew and Arabic laid out right to left.
  • Uses colour to mean only one thing each, and never as the only way information is conveyed.

Known limitations

Documents you upload are shown as their original PDFs, and a scanned PDF may not be readable by a screen reader. The text Covera reads from them is available in the app as ordinary text.
